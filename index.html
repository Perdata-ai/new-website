The problem

A billion people are lonely. The platforms that promised to connect us made it worse.

Social media is optimized for time on app, not time with friends. The result is a public-health crisis the existing platforms are structurally unable to fix.

Our position

Friends, not feeds.

Three rules we will never break.

01

No ads.

We will never sell your attention. Our customers are the people who run events and communities, not advertisers.

02

No bots.

Every account is a real person, verified. Synthetic engagement has no place in a network meant for friendship.

03

No algorithm.

You choose what to see. Not a model trained to keep you scrolling. There is no infinite feed. There never will be.
