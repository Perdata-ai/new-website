The market

A billion lonely people. A $300B problem. No real solution yet.

The loneliness epidemic is the most under-addressed public health crisis of our time. The platforms that promised to connect us optimized for time-on-app instead. We are building the alternative.

Public health

1B+ people report feeling lonely.

The WHO has designated loneliness a global health threat. The U.S. Surgeon General issued a formal advisory. 800,000 deaths a year are linked to social isolation.

Economics

$300B in annual economic cost.

Lost productivity, healthcare spend, and turnover. Mid-market employers now pay for retention and belonging programs as table stakes. That is a direct monetization path for Conexus.

Timing

The backlash is now a market.

AI has commoditized matching. Regulation is catching up to social media. Consumer appetite for something different — something real — has never been higher.

The ecosystem

Four products. One philosophy. Multiple revenue lines.

Each product stands on its own and opens a distinct revenue channel. Together they create a defensible, cross-product data moat through the Conexus Profile.

B2B SaaS — events & communities

Conexus

QR-code AI matching at events and inside communities. Organizers pay per seat or per event. Currently in pilot with ACS, a corporate HR team, and an HOA. Live at getconexus.com.

B2B licensing — health & government

Social Compass

A self-assessment and toolkit for climbing out of social isolation. Free for individuals, licensed to health systems, employers, and government programs. Live at prosocialconsortium.com.

B2C subscription

Conexus Personal

A friendship relationship manager. Like a CRM, but for your real relationships. Mobile app, in beta. Subscription revenue. No ads, no data sales, ever.

B2C — network layer

Conexus Social

An encrypted, friends-only social network. No discover tab, no bots, no ads. Revenue from Conexus Personal subscribers. The network effect that locks the ecosystem together.
