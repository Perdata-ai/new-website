The ecosystem

Four products. One philosophy. One ecosystem.

Each product stands on its own. Together, they cover the full arc of friendship; finding new people, learning how to connect, keeping the friends you have, and sharing with them without the algorithmic noise.

Product 01 — the intro

Conexus

Turn any event or community into a place to meet a friend.

Live at getconexus.com. Currently in pilot with ACS, a corporate HR team, and an HOA community.

  • Organizer drops a QR code.One link, five minutes of setup, works for any event or community of any size.
  • Attendees share what they care about.A short interest profile. No name, photo, or demographic details required — those are revealed only when both people choose.
  • AI matches a worth-meeting person.Not a swipe deck and not a separate feed. Just a quiet nudge to walk across the room.
  • Talk in person. Decide what comes next.Connect after the event or not. The user owns the data, the relationship, and the next step.
Product 02 — getting out of isolation

Social Compass

A library and toolkit for people climbing out of social isolation.

Available at prosocialconsortium.com. Free for individuals, licensed to health systems, employers, and governments.

  • Vetted educational content.Articles, podcasts, and videos chosen for substance — not engagement.
  • A validated self-assessment.A simple check on where you sit on the isolation spectrum, age- and context-aware.
  • Targeted next-step tools.What to do this week, given where you are right now and what you have access to.
  • Distinct pathways.Teens, older adults, post-divorce, post-relocation — different starting points, different paths.
Product 03 — investing in our friendships

Conexus Personal

A friendship relationship manager. Like a customer relationship manager, but the only revenue is your friendship.

Mobile app: Apple App Store and Google Play Store. In beta, coming soon.

  • Tiered relationships.Use The Connection Continuum or your own model. Closer friendships get more frequent connections. Acquaintances, less frequent but not forgotten.
  • Activity page.Birthdays, anniversaries, follow-ups and the people you owe a call to today.
  • Circles.Group friends by shared interest, automatically or by hand, you decide. They never see how you organize them.
  • Memory and history.Every interaction logged for you. Never shared with anyone but you. The data never leaves your control.
Product 04 — the real social network

Conexus Social

The only social network in our stack. Friends only.

Encrypted social network backend. Tightly integrated with Conexus Personal — same identity, same circles.

  • Mutual connection only.You don’t accept requests. You both already know each other in Conexus Personal.
  • End-to-end encrypted.Your posts are not training data. Not now. Not later.
  • No public profile.There is no discover tab. The only people who see your post are your friends you decide to share with.
  • No ads. No bots.Bots cannot join. Ads cannot be sold. The product is the network, not the user.
The connective tissue

Two more pieces. Invisible, but the system depends on them.

Data

Conexus Profile

A portable, encrypted store of who you are. Other apps request access. You approve and nothing leaves until you say so. The foundation under every other product in the stack.

Agent

Conexus Overload (working name)

An AI agent that watches your friends’ shared signals and tells you when to reach out. Not a feed. A nudge: “She just landed her new job, reach out and congratulate.”

From Perdata

The Friendship Event Kit.

Not a product — a resource. Everything an organizer needs to run a friendship-focused event: a facilitation guide, conversation starters, setup instructions and best practices drawn from our pilots. Free to download and use.
